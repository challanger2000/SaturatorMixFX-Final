# Jensen JT-11P-1 THD Curve Digitization — Provisional

Source:
Jensen JT-11P-1 manufacturer datasheet, page 2.

The page contains:
- THD+N versus frequency at fixed +4/+14/+20 dBu input levels;
- THD+N versus input level at fixed 20/30/50 Hz.

Two points are treated as high-confidence tabular anchors:
- +4 dBu / 20 Hz -> 0.025% typical THD;
- +20 dBu / 20 Hz -> 1% typical THD threshold.

All other current points are conservative visual readings from the plotted curves and are marked MANUAL_GRAPH_DIGITIZATION with 30-40% relative uncertainty.

These provisional points are suitable to reject obviously wrong magnetic-curve shapes and guide the next fit, but they are not final precision fitting data.

Final fit promotion requires calibrated graph-coordinate extraction or an equivalent higher-precision source.
